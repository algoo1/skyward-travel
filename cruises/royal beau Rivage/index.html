About this cruise

Category: 5‑Star Luxury Floating Hotel — Route: Luxor ⇄ Aswan

Sail through the heart of Egypt aboard MS Royal Beau Rivage, a 5‑star deluxe Nile Cruise offering elegant accommodation, modern amenities, and personalized hospitality. With its refined décor, spacious cabins, and panoramic Nile views, the Royal Beau Rivage ensures a luxurious journey between Luxor and Aswan that blends comfort, culture, and relaxation.

Itinerary

Typical itineraries range from 3‑, 4‑, or 7‑night programs between Luxor and Aswan, including professionally guided tours to Egypt’s top historical sites:

  • Karnak and Luxor Temples
  • Valley of the Kings & Temple of Hatshepsut
  • Kom Ombo and Edfu Temples
  • Philae Temple & the High Dam
  • Optional Abu Simbel excursion available

Each voyage includes full‑board accommodation, daily excursions, and expert Egyptologist guides for an enriching experience.

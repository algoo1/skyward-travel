Itinerary

The Le Fayan Nile Cruise offers 3‑night, 4‑night, and 7‑night itineraries between Luxor and Aswan, featuring guided visits to Egypt’s most iconic landmarks:

  • Karnak and Luxor Temples
  • Valley of the Kings & Temple of Hatshepsut
  • Edfu Temple & Kom Ombo Temple
  • Philae Temple & the High Dam

All tours are conducted by professional Egyptologists, ensuring a rich and immersive cultural experience throughout your stay.

About this cruise

Category: 5‑Star Luxury Cruise — Route: Luxor ⇄ Aswan — Duration: 3 to 7 Nights

Sail aboard the Royal Elite Nile Cruise, one of Egypt’s most luxurious floating hotels, combining traditional Egyptian design with modern elegance. Every detail has been crafted to offer comfort, sophistication, and unforgettable Nile views from Luxor to Aswan.

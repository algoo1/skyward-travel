About this cruise

Mövenpick Royal Lotus is a 5-star luxury Nile cruise operated by Mövenpick Hotels & Resorts. It sails between Luxor and Aswan on 3, 4, and 7-night itineraries, offering refined dining, panoramic lounges, and serene sun deck experiences.

  • Category: 5-Star Luxury Cruise
  • Operator: Mövenpick Hotels & Resorts
  • Route: Luxor ⇄ Aswan
  • Duration: 3, 4, or 7 nights

Technical Specifications

  • Built by Israel Shipyards
  • Length: 72 m — Width: 14.40 m — Draft: 1.44 m
  • Cruising speed: 14 knots
  • Electricity: 220V (Adapters available upon request)

Accommodation

  • Deluxe cabins located on upper decks
  • Royal Suites at front deck with panoramic Nile views
  • Panoramic windows, en‑suite bathrooms, individual climate control
  • Daily housekeeping and evening turndown service

Dining & Facilities

  • Fine dining restaurant serving international and Egyptian cuisine
  • Panoramic lounge & bar for sunset drinks
  • Sun deck pool and dedicated jacuzzi area
  • Fully equipped fitness center
  • Wi‑Fi available in public areas
  • Onboard gift shop (souvenirs & essentials)

Sample Itinerary (3 Nights)

  • Day 1 — Aswan: Embarkation, Philae Temple visit, optional Nubian Village.
  • Day 2 — Kom Ombo & Edfu: Temple of Kom Ombo, sail to Edfu, Temple of Horus.
  • Day 3 — Luxor West Bank: Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut Temple, Colossi of Memnon.
  • Day 4 — Luxor: Disembarkation. Optional East Bank (Karnak & Luxor Temples).

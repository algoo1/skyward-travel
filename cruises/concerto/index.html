About this cruise

Category: 5‑Star Deluxe Floating Hotel

Launched: September 2008 — Renovated: December 2015

Experience the timeless beauty of the Nile aboard Concerto Nile Cruise, one of the largest and most luxurious ships sailing between Luxor and Aswan. Combining modern elegance with authentic Egyptian charm, the Concerto promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of ancient Egypt.

Itinerary

Sails between Luxor and Aswan with guided visits to signature sites:

  • Karnak and Luxor Temples
  • Valley of the Kings & Temple of Hatshepsut
  • Edfu Temple & Kom Ombo Temple
  • Philae Temple & the High Dam

All tours are led by professional Egyptologists and include full onboard service throughout your stay.

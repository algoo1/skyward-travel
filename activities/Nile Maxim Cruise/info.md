🚢 Nile Maxim Dinner Cruise – Cairo

Category: Luxury Floating Restaurant
Location: Zamalek, Cairo (in front of Marriott Hotel & Omar Khayyam Casino)
Operator: Marriott Cairo
Experience Type: Nile Dinner Cruise with Live Entertainment

Enjoy an unforgettable evening aboard the Nile Maxim, one of Cairo’s most prestigious floating restaurants.
Combining fine dining, authentic Egyptian entertainment, and the mesmerizing views of the Nile, it offers a true luxury night experience in the heart of Cairo.

⚙️ Boat Specifications

Capacity: Up to 240 guests per deck

Decks: Multi-level air-conditioned floating restaurant

Built & Operated by: Marriott Hotel Cairo

Facilities: Panoramic dining halls, bar area, stage, and outdoor terrace

Parking & Access: Private parking and valet service available

🍽️ Dining & Services

Fine dining experience featuring Egyptian & International cuisines

Buffet and à la carte options including appetizers, grilled dishes, desserts, and hot/cold beverages

Live kitchen stations and gourmet dessert displays

High-end service team with personalized hospitality

Reservation required, especially during weekends and public holidays

🎭 Entertainment Program

Traditional Tanoura Dance with colorful whirling performance

Live Oriental Band and belly dancing show

Western music performances by a live band and DJ

Experience the magical Cairo skyline illuminated over the Nile during the cruise

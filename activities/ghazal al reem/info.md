🍽️ Dining Experience

The menu at Ghazal El Reem celebrates freshness, flavor, and tradition.
Guests can enjoy a wide variety of Egyptian and Mediterranean dishes prepared with high-quality ingredients.

Highlights include:

Grilled Lamb Chops: Charcoal-grilled with oriental herbs and a smoky finish.

Egyptian Koshari: A beloved classic, elevated with signature sauces and crispy onions.

Seafood Specials: Fresh sea bass and grilled calamari with sautéed vegetables.

Vegetarian Options: Falafel, vine leaves, and fresh mezzes — light and healthy.

Appetizers: Tabbouleh, hummus, tahini, and oriental dips.
Desserts: Om Ali and golden baklava.

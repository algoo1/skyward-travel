🍽️ Dining Experience

At Kebabgy, grilling is not just cooking — it’s a show of flavor and fire.
Guests enjoy watching chefs prepare their meals live over charcoal flames, ensuring every bite is fresh, juicy, and perfectly seasoned.

Signature Dishes:

Lamb Chops – tender, smoky, and infused with oriental spices

Kofta & Kebab Mix – classic Egyptian grills cooked to perfection

Shish Tawouk – marinated chicken skewers, rich in aroma and taste

Oriental Sausage (Sogo’) – a local favorite full of spice and depth

Appetizers & Sides:

Hummus, baba ghanoush, vine leaves, oriental salads

Fresh baladi bread, basmati rice, sautéed vegetables, golden fries

Desserts:

Om Ali, Kunafa, Baklava – sweet endings with authentic flavor

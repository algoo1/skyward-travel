🚢 Blue Nile Floating Complex – Cairo


Category: Entertainment & Dining Cruise (Fixed Venue)
Location: 9 Saray El Gezirah Street, Zamalek, Cairo
Operator: Blue Nile Group
Experience Type: Floating Restaurants & Nightlife Complex on the Nile

The Blue Nile is one of Cairo’s most famous fixed floating complexes, combining fine dining, live entertainment, and panoramic Nile views.
Located in Zamalek, it serves as a top nightlife destination for both locals and tourists looking for a stylish evening experience by the river.


⚙️ Boat Specifications


Type: Stationary floating complex (does not sail)

Structure: Multi-deck platform with restaurants, lounges, and event halls

Capacity: Several hundred guests across multiple venues

Design: Indoor air-conditioned areas and open-air Nile-view terraces

Accessibility: Valet parking and private car park available


🍽️ Dining & Services


Multiple restaurants offering Egyptian, Mediterranean, and International cuisines

Menu options include mezzes, grilled dishes, seafood, oriental desserts, and cocktails

Vegetarian and halal selections available

Some venues serve alcoholic beverages — a rare feature in Cairo

Ideal for private events, birthdays, and corporate functions

Professional service staff and welcoming ambiance


🎭 Entertainment Program


Live music every evening from 11:00 PM to 2:00 AM

Oriental dance and Tanoura shows on select nights

Resident DJ and international music performances

Perfect venue for nightlife lovers and group gatherings

Panoramic Nile views add a magical touch to the experience

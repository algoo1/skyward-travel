🏙️ Cairo Festival City & Cairo Festival City Mall – New Cairo

Category: Luxury Residential, Business & Retail Destination
Location: Ring Road, East Cairo – New Cairo District
Developer: Al-Futtaim Group
Experience Type: Integrated Living, Shopping & Leisure Hub

Cairo Festival City (CFC) is one of Egypt’s most prestigious mixed-use developments, combining residential villas, business offices, and one of the largest shopping malls in the country.
It offers a complete modern lifestyle experience with premium retail, entertainment, dining, and community services all in one place.

🏡 Residential & Business Complex

Total area: Over 3 million square meters

Residential zones: Oriana Villas, Festival Living Apartments, and Aura Residences

Administrative hubs: The Podium 1 & 2 – modern office spaces for multinational companies

Master-planned with landscaped gardens, playgrounds, schools, and a private marina

24/7 security, maintenance services, and fiber-optic internet

Dedicated underground and outdoor parking facilities

🛍️ Cairo Festival City Mall

Home to 300+ international and local stores

Flagship IKEA store (largest in Egypt & North Africa)

Global fashion brands: ZARA, Massimo Dutti, H&M, Marks & Spencer, Debenhams, LC Waikiki, Nike

Electronics outlets: Samsung, Xiaomi, Red Tag

Carrefour Hypermarket and multiple bank branches (CIB, QNB, etc.)

Galaxy Cinemas – latest movie releases with VIP halls and recliner seating

The famous Festival Fountain – Cairo’s largest musical fountain show

The Village: outdoor dining promenade around the fountain with cafes and restaurants like Starbucks, Costa, Paul, Casper & Gambini’s, Ovio, Tamara, Mori Sushi, Bosporus, Crave, DipnDip, Olives

Family entertainment: Magic Planet, Bounce, Grip n Climb, puppet shows, and children’s workshops

🧾 Mall Facilities & Services

Free Wi-Fi throughout the mall

Mother & baby care rooms, prayer areas, and luggage storage

Services for seniors and mobility support

Lost & Found office open 24/7

Power bank rental and information kiosks

Valet parking and multi-level car parks with direct access to entrances

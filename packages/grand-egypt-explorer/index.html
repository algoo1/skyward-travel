Itinerary

Day 1 – Cairo (Pyramids & Grand Egyptian Museum)
  • Arrival at Cairo Airport.
  • Visit the Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx.
  • Visit the Grand Egyptian Museum.
  • Check-in at the hotel.

Day 2 – Alexandria (Day Trip)
  • Visit the Library of Alexandria.
  • Visit the Greek Orthodox Church of Saint Saba.
  • Visit the Cavafy Museum (House of the Greek poet Cavafy).
  • Visit Qaitbay Citadel.
  • Lunch included.

Day 3 – Alexandria → Fayoum
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Departure to Fayoum.
  • Visit Lake Qarun.
  • Visit Wadi Al-Hitan (Valley of the Whales).
  • Overnight in Fayoum.

Day 4 – Fayoum → Aswan (Cruise Begins)
  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Departure to Aswan.
  • Board the cruise ship.
  • Visit Philae Temple.
  • Visit the High Dam.
  • Overnight on the cruise ship.

Day 5 – Aswan → Kom Ombo → Edfu
  • Sail to Kom Ombo.
  • Visit Kom Ombo Temple.
  • Sail to Edfu.
  • Visit Edfu Temple (Temple of Horus).
  • Overnight on the cruise ship.

Day 6 – Edfu → Luxor (East Bank)
  • Sail to Luxor.
  • Visit Karnak Temple.
  • Visit Luxor Temple.
  • Overnight on the cruise ship in Luxor.

Day 7 – Luxor (West Bank) – End of Cruise
  • Visit Valley of the Kings.
  • Visit Queen Hatshepsut Temple.
  • Visit Colossi of Memnon.
  • End of cruise and departure to airport or continue with next program.

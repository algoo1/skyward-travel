🌅 Program: Luxor & Aswan Tour
🗓 Day 1:

Arrival in Aswan and embarkation on the Nile Cruise.

Visit the High Dam and Philae Temple.

Visit the Unfinished Obelisk.

Dinner and overnight on board in Aswan.

🗓 Day 2:

Sail to Kom Ombo and visit the Temple of Kom Ombo.

Continue sailing to Edfu.

Visit the Temple of Horus in Edfu.

Lunch and overnight on board.

🗓 Day 3:

Sail to Luxor.

Visit the Karnak Temple and the Luxor Temple.

Lunch and overnight on board in Luxor.

🗓 Day 4:

Visit the Valley of the Kings.

Visit the Temple of Queen Hatshepsut.

Visit the Colossi of Memnon.

Disembark from the cruise and transfer to the airport for departure.

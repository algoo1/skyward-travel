About this property

Situated on El Gezirah Island directly on the Nile, this 5‑star hotel features a Nile‑view infinity pool, a full‑service spa and fitness center, and a wide selection of restaurants and bars. Rooms offer panoramic river views and modern amenities. The location provides easy access to downtown Cairo and Zamalek, and is highly rated by couples.

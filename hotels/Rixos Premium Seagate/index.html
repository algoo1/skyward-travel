About this property

Experience world-class service at Rixos Premium SeagateThis luxurious 5-star resort in Sharm El Sheikh is located on the seafront among palm trees. Featuring a private beach and aqua park, Rixos Premium Seagate offers a Premium Ultra All Inclusive Concept. It offers 8 swimming pools including 1 indoor pool,1 buffet restaurant,9 à-la-carte restaurants, and 11 bars. It includes free WiFi on the entire property. All rooms feature a balcony or a terrace with a pool or a garden view. Each one offers a flat-screen TV, a safety box, and a minibar, refilled daily for free. The bathroom includes a shower and slippers. Guests can enjoy an authentic Turkish, Far East, Seafood, and Brazilian dining experience at Lalezar, Asian, Salt, La Churrascaria restaurants. L’Olivo restaurant serves Italian cuisine from à la carte menu, while Epicure offers French and Mangal restaurant serves Bbq on the beach. Rixos’ spa offers a range of relaxation options, including full-body massages, facials, Turkish baths, steam rooms, a therapy saloon, a beauty center, and a Ladies' Spa. There is also a modern gym equipped with state-of-the-art cardiovascular equipment. At Rixos Premium Seagate you will find a tennis court. Other facilities offered at the property include a nightclub, luggage storage, and shops (on-site). An array of activities can be enjoyed on site or in the surroundings, including diving, snorkeling, and beach football, also our Exclusive Sports Club offers a range of workout programs like CrossFit, spinning, Pilates, Aqua Bike, Aqua Jumping, and gravity Yoga. Unique kids concept of Rixy Club with a spacious area, game rooms, special kids restaurant and 2 kids swimming pools, and Teenage Club from the age of 13. Sharm El Sheikh International Airport is 9 km away from Rixos Premium Seagate. Guests can do shopping at Naa’ma Bay Shopping Centre which is 18 km away. An airport shuttle is available upon request. Rixos Aquaventure Park is one of the most diverse and comprehensive water parks in the region. There are 6 towers with 23 water slides, large interactive Water Fountains, Aqua Tower, Lazy River and the Wave Pool.

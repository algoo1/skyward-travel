About this propertyLocated on the beach in the city of Hurghada, Sunrise Aqua Joy Resort is a family hotel that offers 6 outdoor pools, park facilities and private beach access. It offers free WiFi in public areas. The 4-star hotel features air-conditioned rooms furnished with a simple décor and warm colours. Each room comes with a minibar, a satellite TV and has a balcony or terrace. There is a private bathroom. Sunrise Aqua Joy Resort offers International, Italian, Asian, Egyptian, Greek, and Seafood restaurants.The property also has 5 bars, including a shisha bar. The hotel features facilities such as an aqua park, a gym, spa and beauty salon. Hurghada International Airport is 6.5 km from the hotel, and the bus station is a 10-minute walk away.

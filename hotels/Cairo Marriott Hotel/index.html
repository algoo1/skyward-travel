About this property

Set on Zamalek Island within a historic palace complex, this 5‑star hotel offers lush gardens, an outdoor pool, on‑site casino, and a range of restaurants and bars. Rooms feature Nile or garden views with modern amenities, alongside a fitness center and spa services. The location provides quick access to downtown Cairo and cultural attractions.

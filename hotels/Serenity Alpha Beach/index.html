About this propertyLocated on a private beach, Serenity Beach Resort is a Moroccan-style resort 24 mi south of Hurghada. It offers 5-star accommodations with Red Sea views with 5 swimming pools and 3 restaurants. The Breach features a coral reef accessible through a jetty. All the rooms include terrace or a large balcony with views of the garden, pool or the sea. They have modern wood furnishings and come equipped with satellite TV, air conditioning and tea-and-coffee making facilities. The resort has a Cuisine of the al carte restaurants (Moroccan, Mexican and Indian), pool and beach bars that offers refreshing beverages. Sea Food menu available in all a la carte restaurants. Serenity guests can enjoy a relaxing massage or play a game of tennis on the flood-lit court. The hotel has a diving center on site and is 1640 feet from the Madinat-Makadi Golf Couse. Serenity Beach Resort is 22 mi from Hurghada International Airport.

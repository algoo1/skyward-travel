About this property

Located in San Stefano, Alexandria, this 5‑star beachfront resort offers private beach access, indoor and outdoor pools, a full‑service spa and fitness center, and multiple restaurants and bars. Rooms feature sea views and elegant finishes. The property connects to San Stefano complex and marina, with couples highly rating the location for seaside walks and shopping.

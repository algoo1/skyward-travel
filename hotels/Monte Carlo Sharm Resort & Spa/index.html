About this property

Set along the shores of Sharm El Sheikh, this resort combines private beach access, multiple outdoor pools and a full‑service spa with a variety of restaurants and bars. Rooms and suites feature Red Sea views and modern amenities, with water sports, family facilities and evening entertainment available on site.

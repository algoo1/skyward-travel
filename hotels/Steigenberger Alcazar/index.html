About this propertySteigenberger Alcazar is the latest venue in Nabq around 15 minutes from Sharm El-Sheikh International Airport. This five-star resort enjoys 30 000 sqm of its own private beach with underwater scenery and 3 swimming pools which are heated during the winter. Guests can enjoy free WiFi throughout the property and in the rooms. Steigenberger Alcazar offers 584 guest rooms. Superior Rooms and Deluxe Family Rooms stand just off the beach while Villas and Junior Suites accommodate larger groups. The Alcazar offers two main restaurants serving breakfast, lunch and dinner and five specialty a la carte restaurants. In addition there are two lobby bars, pool bars and a beach bar. For children, the animation team guarantees fun and activities from the moment they arrive either in their private pools or at the fully supervised kids club. Adults can enjoy multiple treatments in Mividaspa. Ras Mohammed National Park is 34 mi from Steigenberger Alcazar, while center of Nabq Bay is 1312 ft from the property.
